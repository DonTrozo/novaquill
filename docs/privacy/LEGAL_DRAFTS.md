## Privacy notice draft

NovaQuill at novaquill.co.za provides browser document editing and ordinary electronic signatures. The confirmed legal entity/contact above is responsible for account/service data. Where customers determine the purposes of processing information in their documents, NovaQuill processes that information under the customer's lawful instructions as operator.

Information includes account/contact identifiers and authentication records; service/subscription and payment references; documents and text/signatures users choose to process; saved signature images/names when explicitly requested; and limited security/rights-request records. Documents may contain third parties' personal or special personal information. Customers must have authority and a lawful basis to process those records; uploading does not create that authority.

The service uses information to provide requested editing/signing/storage, authenticate accounts, administer subscriptions, protect the service and handle rights requests/legal obligations. Record the applicable lawful basis for each purpose in the processing register; do not rely on blanket consent. Reusable signature storage is optional and requires an affirmative choice. Reuse requires the signer's deliberate selection and placement. Document contents/signatures are not supplied to unrelated advertising or AI training by this application.

Ordinary editing/finalisation takes place in the browser. The existing sign-in redirect temporarily preserves a browser draft. Pro signed-document cloud storage takes place under the existing download flow. Hosting/storage/authentication/payment providers receive only the categories necessary for their role, as listed in the completed provider inventory. Identify actual recipients, locations, security arrangements and section 72 transfer grounds before publication. Do not claim South African-only storage or encrypted backups without evidence.

Explain the confirmed retention schedule for account records, saved signatures, cloud documents, billing, operational security records, privacy requests and provider backups. A saved signature can be removed independently of completed PDFs. Account deletion and self-service document deletion follow the service controls; lawful exceptions or independently retained agreements require specific justification and restricted handling. Do not promise to delete third-party copies or provider backups instantly.

Account holders can export their data, delete saved signatures/documents/account and submit free access, correction, objection or deletion requests in advanced dashboard controls. Other data subjects can use the confirmed public privacy contact. Verify identity proportionately and explain outcomes/any justified restrictions in writing. Give the Information Regulator complaint channel and the published PAIA manual location. Do not make marketing consent a condition of the signing service; no marketing campaign/consent store has been added by this work.

## Operator agreement drafting schedule

Complete the parties, effective date, authority and customer instructions; do not fabricate signatures or dates. The written agreement should establish:

- Roles, permitted processing purposes, duration, categories, data subjects and confidentiality obligations.
- Processing only on documented lawful instructions; no unrelated reuse, training, advertising or undisclosed disclosures.
- Section 19 safeguards, account-scoped access, encryption/key management, provider/backups controls and review/testing duties.
- Approved subprocessors, written equivalent protections, transfer locations and a recorded section 72 basis.
- Assistance with access/correction/objection/deletion requests, retention instructions, return/deletion and any justified legal restriction.
- Immediate operator notification to the responsible party on the statutory threshold, cooperation, evidence preservation and allocation of regulator/data-subject notifications.
- Assurance/audit information, staff access, incident contacts, termination, approved retention and data disposal/backup treatment.

Verify these promises against the actual providers before signing. An unexecuted template is not the section 21 written arrangement.
