## Findings from published terms

Vercel's DPA reviewed here was updated 17 March 2026, effective 31 March 2026; it states its processor scope for Pro/Enterprise. That does not prove an agreement for an unverified plan. Cloudinary's DPA is tied to its subscription agreement/order. Its public subprocessor list identifies US/global services and availability of other environments. Those statements do not establish the specific region for NovaQuill.

Do not claim that a .co.za domain, encryption, an EU SCC, a provider's marketing statement, or a downloadable DPA alone completes POPIA section 72 analysis. Record the actual recipient, onward recipients, information categories, destination, binding terms, applicable protections and enforceability. Assess whether section 72(1)(a) or another specific permitted ground applies. Evaluate any additional special-information/children/prior-authorisation requirements for the intended use. Escalate unresolved issues rather than recording an invented consent or asserting South African-only storage.

## Completion record per provider

Record: account/project identifier; contracting parties; service/plan; agreement/DPA version and acceptance evidence; source URL and document hash; processing purpose/categories; regions and support-access locations; authorised subprocessors/onward transfers; encryption/key ownership; retention/backup deletion; incident notice commitment; rights-request assistance; admin roles/MFA; reviewer/date/next review; conclusion and unresolved restrictions.

Restrict the supporting evidence. Do not commit credentials, private signed agreements, government IDs or raw user documents. `compliance/evidence.json` may contain opaque evidence references, review dates and hashes only.
