## Instructions and lawful use

The customer defines the purposes, categories, data subjects and authorised service instructions in Schedule A. NovaQuill processes the entrusted information only on documented lawful instructions and as necessary to provide those agreed services, unless applicable law requires otherwise. Where legally permitted, notify the customer before processing required by such law. ## Confidentiality and access

Personnel and service providers may access entrusted information only to the extent required for authorised duties, under confidentiality and access restrictions. Use individual administrative accounts, least privilege, appropriate authentication and timely removal of access. Prohibit unauthorised disclosure, copying and sharing. Application access to stored documents and signatures is account scoped and checked on the server; administrators require a separate justified operational basis.

## Rights assistance and accuracy

Provide reasonable assistance so the customer can answer lawful access, correction, objection, restriction and deletion requests. Promptly refer requests about customer-controlled records to the customer's designated contact where necessary. Verify identity and authority proportionately before disclosing or altering records. Do not unilaterally alter an executed agreement merely because a data subject disputes a field; document the appropriate correction/provenance process and any lawful restriction. Keep request/outcome/notification records with justified retention.

## Security compromises

If there are reasonable grounds to believe entrusted personal information has been accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person, NovaQuill immediately notifies the customer in its role as operator, provides available facts and updates, mitigates further exposure and preserves proportionate evidence. The customer coordinates responsible-party notifications under section 22 unless a different legal role applies. Neither party postpones required notice merely until the entire investigation is complete. Contacts, escalation, containment, regulator/data-subject procedures and recordkeeping are specified in Schedule D. No universal 72-hour statutory deadline is asserted.

## Retention, return and disposal

Process entrusted information for the agreed duration/purpose and follow lawful retention/deletion instructions. On termination or a valid instruction, return or delete it as agreed, including service copies and applicable provider procedures. Where a law or justified independent basis requires specific retention, document the record, basis, restriction, access and review/disposal date; inform the customer where legally permitted. Preserve properly recorded legal/incident holds, review them and release them when no longer justified. Explain backup deletion/restoration treatment and limitations accurately; do not promise instant removal of every independent copy. Removing a reusable signature does not rewrite completed PDFs.

## Assurance and cooperation

Maintain information sufficient to demonstrate the agreed controls, cooperate with reasonable assessments and regulator enquiries, and communicate material protection deficiencies. Use confidentiality and proportionate access arrangements so assurance does not expose other customers' records, secrets or security vulnerabilities. Material deficiencies require documented remediation, restrictions or cessation of affected processing where necessary. Review this agreement when purposes, providers, locations or legal obligations change.

## Service-specific interpretation and change control

The service covered here is NovaQuill's browser editing/finalisation, deliberate signature placement, optional account signature storage and existing Pro PDF storage. It does not include an unimplemented signing invitation workflow, accredited advanced signatures, document-content AI processing or general identity verification. A transaction/customer schedule may narrow this scope but cannot silently add unrelated processing.

Instructions include the customer's authorised service actions within the agreed scope. Additional instructions, export/return formats, authorised contacts and disputed instructions must be documented. Keep changes to instructions and approved providers traceable; notify the customer before affected processing where required, with a reasonable process for concerns or lawful cessation. No inspection or audit may expose another customer's information or production secrets.

The processor relationship does not authorise use of customer document contents for independent service profiling, advertising or model training. Account/billing/security information processed for NovaQuill's own purposes must have its own lawful basis and transparent notice. Case retention or a legal restriction does not grant a perpetual content licence.

The unresolved account-specific provider and backup entries in Schedules B/C remain conditions for execution.
